### 1. Local sections and smooth descent — implemented and checked

Prove that a map with local smooth right inverses detects smoothness of maps out of its
target. Then construct such sections from suitable submersion normal forms. This directly
supports the `smooth_descend` field of `HomogeneousQuotientRealization`.

Initial implementation: [LocalSections.lean](../LieGroups/Manifold/LocalSections.lean).
It defines `LieGroups.Manifold.HasContMDiffLocalSections` and proves surjectivity, descent of
`C^n` regularity, and the composition criterion for a `C^n` projection. It uses only Mathlib,
works over a nontrivially normed field, and allows different source/target model spaces.
It assumes the local sections.

[SubmersionLocalSections.lean](../LieGroups/Manifold/SubmersionLocalSections.lean) constructs
a local section through any given point at which Mathlib's `IsSubmersionAt` holds, and
deduces local sections everywhere for a surjective submersion. The source model is
boundaryless. This normal-form consequence needs neither finite-dimensionality nor
completeness, and works over a nontrivially normed field. It does not derive the normal
form from a differential condition, construct quotient manifolds, or discharge the
current global realization assumptions.

The statement anchors are Lee, Theorem 4.26 (the forward direction), and Theorem 4.29.
The chart-normal-form input is supplied by Stage 2 under its differential hypotheses.

Reproduction: `lake build`, then `lake env lean scripts/AuditFoundations.lean`.

Acceptance: the section takes the chosen target point to the prescribed source point,
is `C^n` there, and satisfies the right-inverse identity on a neighborhood. Build and audit
both new declarations, and keep them independent of BF–RF code.

### 2. Differential criteria and local product charts — implemented and checked

Bridge a split-surjective differential to the submersion normal form in the required
boundaryless setting, using existing inverse-function tools where possible. Establish
the finite-dimensional real specialization and identify every completeness/regularity
hypothesis rather than silently moving between differential and normal-form definitions.
Principal anchor: Lee, Theorem 4.12, specifically its submersion case.

Acceptance: local sections can be obtained from the differential criterion that will be
proved for a quotient projection, without assuming the desired charts.

First milestone implemented in
[SubmersionDifferential.lean](../LieGroups/Manifold/SubmersionDifferential.lean):

- Over `ℝ` or `ℂ` (`RCLike`), for a complete source normed space and `n ≠ 0`,
  `exists_contDiffAt_localProduct_of_rightInverse` constructs an open partial homeomorphism
  from a `C^n` map at a point and a continuous linear right inverse to its differential.
  Its coordinates are `(f z, z - R (A z))`, with the second coordinate valued in `ker A`.
  The map and its inverse are `C^n` at the base points, and `f` becomes the first projection.
- `exists_contDiffAt_localSection_of_rightInverse` fixes the kernel coordinate to construct
  a local section taking `f x` to `x`. The finite-dimensional-target corollary
  `exists_contDiffAt_localSection_of_surjective` requires only surjectivity of the differential.
  Finite-dimensional real source spaces satisfy the completeness assumption automatically.
- `hasContMDiffLocalSections_of_surjective_splitDifferential` and
  `hasContMDiffLocalSections_of_surjective_differential` connect the pointwise constructions
  to Stage 1 for surjective `C^n` maps of normed spaces, giving access to smooth descent.

The proof reuses Mathlib's `ContinuousLinearEquiv.equivOfRightInverse`,
`ContDiffAt.toOpenPartialHomeomorph`, and `ContDiffAt.to_localInverse`.
It adds no new assumptions to the existing Stage 1 declarations.

The neighborhood and manifold milestone is now implemented:

- `exists_contDiffOn_localInverse` and `exists_contDiffOn_localProduct_of_rightInverse`
  assume `ContDiffOn` on an open set and produce `C^n` maps on the entire open source and target.
  This includes `n = ∞`; no inference from pointwise `C^∞` regularity to a single smooth
  neighborhood is used.
- [SubmersionCriterion.lean](../LieGroups/Manifold/SubmersionCriterion.lean) proves
  `isSubmersionAt_of_rightInverse_mfderiv` by constructing charts in the maximal atlas.
  Its hypotheses are `RCLike 𝕜`, `CompleteSpace E`, `I.Boundaryless`, `IsManifold I n M`,
  `IsManifold J n N`, `ContMDiff I J n f`, `n ≠ 0`, and a continuous linear right inverse
  to `mfderiv I J f x`. No boundaryless assumption on the target model is needed.
- `isSubmersionAt_of_surjective_mfderiv` replaces the right inverse with surjectivity
  when the target model is finite-dimensional. In particular, it applies to smooth maps
  of finite-dimensional real manifolds without boundary; source completeness is then automatic.
- `exists_contMDiffAt_localSection_of_surjective_mfderiv` gives the prescribed-point section.
  `hasContMDiffLocalSections_of_surjective_split_mfderiv` and
  `hasContMDiffLocalSections_of_surjective_mfderiv` provide the smooth-descent interface
  when the map is also surjective.

Statement comparison: Lee's rank theorem concerns smooth maps between finite-dimensional
real manifolds of constant rank. We implement its full-rank submersion conclusion from
surjectivity at the chosen point, not the arbitrary constant-rank theorem. Combining this
with Stage 1 gives the forward local-section implication of Theorem 4.26 and the descent
criterion of Theorem 4.29. The Lean results additionally allow nonzero finite regularity,
complex scalars, and complete infinite-dimensional source models with split differentials.
Prerequisites are Mathlib's inverse function theorem, openness of the continuous linear
equivalences, finite-dimensional linear splitting, and the chart/tangent-space APIs.

### 4. Differentials of homomorphisms and adjoint compatibility — implemented and checked

Prove that differentiating a smooth group homomorphism gives a Lie-algebra homomorphism,
with identity, composition, exponential, and adjoint compatibility. Reuse Mathlib's Lie
algebra conventions. Record left/right action and bracket-sign conventions explicitly.
The first two milestones use Lee, Proposition 8.30, Theorem 8.44, and
Proposition 8.45, checked below. Warner and Helgason remain planned convention
cross-checks; their exact statement locators have not been verified.

Acceptance: differentiating a supplied smooth group equivalence constructs its induced
Lie equivalence and supplies the compatibility field of `IntegratedLieEquiv`, using
the actual downstream structures. For a separately prescribed Lie equivalence, identify
it with the induced one; constructing a group map with that prescribed differential
belongs to Stage 7.

First milestone: [HomomorphismDifferential.lean](../LieGroups/HomomorphismDifferential.lean),
implemented on 2026-10-05.

- `homLieDifferential` is the continuous linear manifold differential at the identity
  of a group homomorphism. For a smooth homomorphism, it transports left-invariant
  fields, respects identity and composition, and maps the constructed one-parameter
  subgroup to the subgroup with differentiated initial velocity. Consequently,
  `lieExp_map` proves `f (lieExp v) = lieExp (homLieDifferential f v)`.
- `lieAdjoint g` differentiates conjugation `x ↦ g * x * g⁻¹`. It is a continuous
  linear map with identity and multiplication laws. `lieExp_adjoint` identifies
  its exponential with conjugation, and `homLieDifferential_adjoint` proves
  naturality under smooth homomorphisms. This fixes the left-translation and
  conjugation conventions used by the subsequent bracket proof. Mathlib's
  bracket is `[V_v,V_w](1)` for left-invariant fields; its coordinate sign is
  `D V_w(V_v) - D V_v(V_w)`.
- This first milestone did not establish bracket preservation or package the
  differential as a `LieHom`. The later milestones below provide the bracket
  construction and downstream `IntegratedLieEquiv` adapter.

### 5. Frobenius and integration of Lie subalgebras — complete

Implemented on 2026-10-18 for finite-dimensional real Hausdorff Lie groups with a
boundaryless model (`I.Boundaryless`). Every Lie subalgebra has a connected Hausdorff
immersed integration with exactly the prescribed tangent Lie algebra. Connected
immersed subgroup images with the same tangent algebra are equal. This is uniqueness
of the ambient subgroup image; a bundled unique diffeomorphism between arbitrary
source presentations is not asserted.

The main entry point is
[`nonempty_lieSubalgebraIntegration`](../LieGroups/LieSubalgebraIntegration.lean).
Its `LieSubalgebraIntegration S` result retains the source group, intrinsic topology,
smooth atlas, connectedness, Hausdorffness, an injective immersive homomorphism, and
`presentation.lieAlgebra = S`. The source model is `lieSubalgebraModelSubmodule S`.
`LieSubalgebraIntegration.subgroup_eq_lieSubalgebraSubgroup` identifies its image with
the algebraic subgroup generated by exponentials of `S`; `subgroup_eq` proves image
uniqueness. No integrating manifold, local flow, integral plaque, or regularity
certificate is an input to the existence theorem.
